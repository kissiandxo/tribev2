ETHICS:
- Never make false claims about the competitor
- Never use the competitor's trademarked name IN the ad copy (only in the keyword targeting)
- Focus on our strengths, not competitor weaknesses
- Be factual: use real differentiators (speed, guarantee, reviews, price, accreditation)

CONQUEST STRATEGY:
When someone searches "[Competitor Name]", your ad appears above their website. The ad must:
1. Immediately signal we're a better/comparable alternative
2. Highlight the #1 reason to switch (response time, price, guarantee, reviews)
3. Contain a strong CTA
4. Fit Google's responsive search ad format (15 headlines x 30 chars, 4 descriptions x 90 chars)
