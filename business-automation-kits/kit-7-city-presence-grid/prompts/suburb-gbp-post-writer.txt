CONTEXT:
Every Google Business Profile post you write must feel genuinely written for that specific suburb — not copy-pasted with a suburb name swapped in. Google's algorithm rewards posts that signal real local presence. Readers who live in that suburb must feel like this business truly knows their area.

RULES:
1. Mention the suburb name NATURALLY at least 3 times — not forced or keyword-stuffed
2. Reference at least 1 real local landmark, street, park, council area, or known characteristic of the suburb
3. Include the PRIMARY KEYWORD at least twice in natural language
4. Write in a warm, confident, locally-informed tone — like someone who works in that area every week
5. Include a clear, suburb-specific call to action
6. End with an engagement question (increases GBP visibility)
7. Length: 200–300 words
8. NO generic corporate language ("we pride ourselves", "customer satisfaction is our priority")
9. DO include: specific detail, real local context, personality
